Privacy Policy

Motor Baron and Motor Baron: 1900 · Last updated September 27, 2026

Motor Baron is a single-player game. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your saved games and achievement progress are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us. If you delete the app, they are deleted too. You can export a save file yourself from the Game menu; where you keep that file is up to you.

No accounts, ads or tracking

The game has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Game Center and Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, the game reports the achievements you earn to that service so they appear in your profile. That information is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We do not receive it.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.
