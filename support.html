Common questions

Where is my game saved? On your device, automatically after every month. To move a company to another device, use Game → Export save, then Game → Import save on the other device.

Can I continue my Motor Baron: 1900 company in the full game? Yes. In Motor Baron: 1900, use Game → Export save. In Motor Baron, use Game → Import save and choose that file. Your company carries on from where you stopped.

Does the game have ads or in-app purchases? No. Neither version has ads, and neither sells anything inside the game.

What does "Undo month" do? It takes back the last month you ended, once, in case you ended a month by mistake.
